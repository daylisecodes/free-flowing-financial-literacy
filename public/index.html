Faith without financial promises

Money is a tool. It is not your foundation.

“But seek first God’s Kingdom and his righteousness; and all these things will be given to you as well.” Matthew 6:33, World English Bible

We use Scripture for spiritual encouragement and stewardship reflection. We do not claim that faith guarantees wealth, debt freedom, investment gains, a mortgage approval, a particular home, or any financial timeline.

What this site does

  • Explains general financial concepts in plain language
  • Uses hypothetical calculators for learning
  • Links to CFPB, SEC Investor.gov, FTC, and other authoritative resources
  • Encourages users to verify information and seek qualified help when needed

What this site does not do

  • Recommend specific investments, loans, lenders, securities, or credit products
  • Predict approvals, credit scores, market returns, or financial outcomes
  • Collect bank credentials, SSNs, credit reports, brokerage logins, or uploaded financial documents
