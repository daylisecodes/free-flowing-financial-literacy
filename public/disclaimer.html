Educational disclaimer

Education, not individualized financial advice

Free Flowing Financial Literacy is an educational project. Nothing on this site should be treated as a personalized recommendation, professional opinion, guarantee, or substitute for advice from a qualified professional who understands your circumstances.

No financial, investment, lending, tax, legal, credit-repair, or real-estate advice

The site does not act as a financial planner, investment adviser, broker-dealer, lender, mortgage broker, housing counselor, credit repair organization, accountant, tax adviser, attorney, insurance professional, or real-estate professional. It does not create a fiduciary or professional-client relationship.

Calculators are simplified illustrations

Calculator outputs use numbers you enter and simplified formulas. They can omit material factors such as taxes, fees, interest-rate changes, underwriting rules, market movement, inflation, insurance, maintenance, transaction costs, product terms, timing, and individual circumstances. They are not quotes, offers, approvals, preapprovals, forecasts, appraisals, credit decisions, or guarantees.

Investing involves risk

No content recommends any security, cryptocurrency, asset, fund, allocation, strategy, or trading action. Hypothetical growth examples are mathematical illustrations only. Real investments can lose value, and past or assumed returns do not guarantee future results.

Home-buying information is general education

Home-buying tools do not determine whether anyone is ready to buy a home or eligible for financing. Real mortgage decisions involve lenders, actual disclosures, underwriting, property information, and many other factors.

Faith content is not a financial promise

Scripture and Christian reflections are included for spiritual encouragement and stewardship. The site does not teach that prayer, giving, tithing, fasting, obedience, or “enough faith” guarantees wealth, debt relief, investment gains, credit improvement, loan approval, or homeownership.

Sources can change

The project aims to use authoritative sources and to update material when needed, but government guidance, laws, product rules, websites, and financial practices can change. Verify important information using current primary sources and qualified professionals before acting.

Do not rely on this website alone for a decision that could materially affect your finances, legal rights, taxes, credit, housing, insurance, or investments.
