Authoritative sources behind the lessons

Financial explanations are paraphrased rather than copied. These references let users verify concepts at the original source. Access dates reflect the project review performed October 8, 2026.

Money foundations

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. (n.d.). Your Money, Your Goals toolkit. Retrieved October 8, 2026, from https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/

Used for general education about tracking income, spending, bills, cash flow, goals, debt, and credit.

Saving & emergency funds

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. (n.d.). An essential guide to building an emergency fund. Retrieved October 8, 2026, from https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/

Used for the definition and purpose of emergency savings, the point that targets depend on individual circumstances, and general saving practices.

Used for credit-report, credit-score, multiple-score, and DTI education.

Home buying & equity

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. (n.d.). Determine your down payment. Retrieved October 8, 2026, from https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/prepare/determine-your-down-payment/
